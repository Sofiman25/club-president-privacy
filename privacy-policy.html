Privacy Policy for Club President

Effective date: March 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Club President ("the App") handles user information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Club President does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The App stores gameplay-related information locally on your device to support features such as save progress, career data, club data, and game settings.

2. How Information Is Used

Local data is used only to provide core game functionality, including saving and loading your progress and maintaining your in-game career and club information.

3. Data Sharing

Club President does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

At this time, the App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or account-based data collection services as part of its core gameplay experience.

5. Data Storage and Security

Game data is stored locally on your device. While reasonable care is taken in the design of the App, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to the App in error, please contact us.

7. Your Choices

Because game data is stored locally on your device, you can remove stored data by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's local storage through your device settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

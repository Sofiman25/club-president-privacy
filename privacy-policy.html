Privacy Policy for Club President

Effective date: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Club President ("the App"), provided by ERTUĞRUL DAĞCI ("we", "us", or "our"), accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information. It also explains the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Local gameplay data

The App stores gameplay information on your device, including saved progress, career and club data, game settings, purchases or entitlements recognized by the App, and other information required to continue your game. This information normally remains on your device unless you choose to use a cloud feature.

1.2 Crash and diagnostic data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes, non-fatal errors, and Application Not Responding (ANR) events. Crashlytics may automatically process stack traces, error messages, relevant application state, App version, device model, operating-system information, device state, Crashlytics installation identifiers, Firebase installation identifiers, and diagnostic keys or logs added by the App. These reports are used to diagnose and improve stability and are not intended to contain names, email addresses, or free-form user content.

1.3 Analytics data

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and how it performs. Depending on device settings, region, and Google service configuration, analytics data may include App interactions, session and usage information, device and App information, identifiers associated with the App installation or device, and general geographic information derived from technical signals such as an IP address. We use this information for analytics, product improvement, and crash-reporting context.

1.4 Advertising data

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and App information, approximate location derived from technical signals, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, secure, and limit advertisements. The ads shown may depend on your region, age-related settings, consent signals, Google account settings, and device advertising settings.

1.5 Google Play Games and cloud saves

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games or use cloud-save and achievement features, Google may process your Play Games player identifier, profile or display information, authentication status, achievement information, and the gameplay save data you choose to upload. Authentication is handled by Google; we do not receive your Google password.

1.6 In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We may receive product identifiers, transaction or purchase tokens, purchase status, and entitlement information needed to verify and provide purchased content. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

1.7 Communications

If you contact us for support, we process the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, message, and any diagnostic information or attachments you send.

2. How We Use Information

We process information to:

  • provide, save, restore, and maintain game functionality;
  • support Play Games, achievements, cloud saves, and purchases;
  • display and measure advertisements;
  • measure App usage and improve gameplay and user experience;
  • detect, diagnose, and fix crashes, ANRs, errors, abuse, and security problems;
  • respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be transmitted to Google as our service provider, or processed by Google and its advertising partners, when you use the services listed below:

  • Firebase Crashlytics;
  • Google Analytics for Firebase;
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob);
  • Google Play Games Services and cloud saves;
  • Google Play Billing.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy practices. For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase, and Google's advertising technologies information.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or our legal rights, to investigate fraud or security incidents, or as part of a business transfer where permitted by law.

4. Legal Bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide requested App features and purchases, for our legitimate interests in maintaining, securing, analyzing, and improving the App, to comply with legal obligations, and on the basis of consent where consent is required. You may withdraw consent through the available consent, Google account, or device controls, without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local game data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.
  • Cloud-save, Play Games, purchase, advertising, and analytics data is retained according to the applicable Google service settings and policies.
  • Firebase states that Crashlytics crash stack traces and associated identifiers are generally retained for 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins.
  • Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, protect legal rights, and meet legal obligations.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information controlled by us by using the contact address below. We will evaluate requests under applicable law and technical limitations. Information controlled directly by Google can be managed through your Google account, Play Games, Google Play, or device settings.

6. Security and International Processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. Data transmitted to supported Google services is protected in transit using industry-standard encryption. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to their legal safeguards and privacy terms.

7. Your Choices

  • Delete local data by clearing App storage or uninstalling the App.
  • Manage Play Games and cloud data through your Google account and Play Games settings.
  • Manage advertising personalization or reset/delete the advertising identifier in Android settings.
  • Manage Google activity and privacy controls through your Google account.
  • Contact us to exercise privacy rights available under applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not ask users to provide a name, phone number, precise location, or payment-card details directly to us. We do not knowingly use the App to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Purchases, advertising, analytics, and Google account features may be subject to age, guardian, regional, Google account, and consent controls. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, service providers, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or store listing when appropriate.
